Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Clarity is a local-first task manager. This policy explains what data we handle — in short: almost none.

Your tasks stay yours

Tasks, reminders, and settings you create in Clarity are stored on your own device. We do not run user accounts, and we do not operate servers that collect or store your task data.

Optional sync you configure

If you connect third-party services in the app (such as Gmail, or your own Supabase project for sync), your data flows directly between your device and those services under their own privacy policies. Clarity never sees or relays that data through infrastructure we control, because there is none.

This website

This landing site is a static page. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and collects no personal information. Download buttons link to GitHub Releases and Google Play, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children

Clarity does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no data at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new revision date.
